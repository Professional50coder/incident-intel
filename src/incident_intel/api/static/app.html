    What this can and cannot do

    • The model is a small autoencoder trained on one scene (UCSD Ped2 walkway). Its frame-level AUC on the Ped2 test set is 0.674: it ranks unusual frames better than chance, but misses and false alarms are common.
    • It looks at single frames at 64x64 grayscale, every 5th frame. It cannot tell a cart from a lighting change; it only says "this moment looks unlike the rest".
    • On other scenes, use clip-relative flagging and treat flags as a review queue, not as confirmed incidents.
